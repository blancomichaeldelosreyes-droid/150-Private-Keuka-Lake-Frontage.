About this property

New lakefront cottage. Your own piece of Keuka Lake.

Experience the beauty of Keuka Lake from this newly constructed lakefront cottage, thoughtfully designed to combine modern comfort with the timeless charm of the Finger Lakes.

Situated on an exceptionally private property with over 150 feet of pristine shoreline, you'll enjoy peaceful surroundings, stunning lake views, and room to relax without the crowds. Wake each morning to the gentle sounds of the water, spend your days swimming, boating, or fishing, and end each evening watching unforgettable sunsets over the lake.

The cottage comfortably accommodates up to six guests with 3 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms. Everything is new, offering a fresh, clean, and comfortable space for your vacation.

Bring your boat and take advantage of the brand-new permanent dock, providing easy access to one of the Finger Lakes' most beautiful destinations. Whether you're planning a family vacation, a couples' getaway, or a relaxing week with friends, you'll find the perfect balance of comfort, privacy, and lakefront living.

Conveniently located on the west side near Keuka Lake's renowned wineries, breweries, restaurants, hiking trails, and charming lakeside towns, this is the ideal home base for creating lasting Finger Lakes memories.

Why this property

What makes it special.

Why they chose this property

Keuka Lake is one of the most beautiful and unique lakes in New York's Finger Lakes region, known for its crystal-clear water, scenic vineyards, and welcoming small-town charm. This cottage is in a peaceful area where guests can enjoy the quiet beauty of the lake while still being a short drive from wineries, breweries, restaurants, hiking trails, and local shops.

What we enjoy most is the privacy and tranquility of the location. Whether you're taking a morning walk along the shoreline, spending the day on the water, or relaxing on the private dock as the sun sets, the pace of life here encourages you to slow down and enjoy every moment.

What makes this property unique

What makes this property truly special is its rare combination of new construction, exceptional privacy, and approximately 150 feet of private Keuka Lake frontage. The cottage features its own private waterfront and private dock, so you'll have your own space to swim, fish, boat, or simply relax by the water.

Unlike many lake rentals that are closely spaced, this cottage sits on a spacious waterfront lot, creating a peaceful setting where you can truly unwind. Everything in the cottage is brand new, offering a fresh, clean, and thoughtfully designed retreat with all the comforts of home.
